I want to take my dad somewhere for four days at the end of May. He's 72, walks a lot, can't do heat, and has never been to Portugal.

Portugal in late May is close to ideal for him: mild, long evenings, and the heat that arrives in July hasn't yet. Lisbon or Porto both fit. Porto is smaller and steeper; Lisbon gives you four days without repeating yourself, and the day trips are easy by train.

Lisbon then. One thing — he doesn't know yet. I'm giving him the plan as a birthday present on the 12th.

Good choice, and a good present. Lisbon in late May is mild, the light is long, and a plan with dates on it is a better present than a vague promise. Tell me how you want the days to feel and I'll lay them out.

Plan the four days. He'll want to see everything, but I want one slow day in there.
