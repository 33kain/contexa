A few directions, depending on what you want the week to feel like. If you want warm weather without peak crowds, southern Europe in May is close to ideal — Portugal, Andalusia, Sicily. If you'd rather walk than sit, the Dolomites and the Scottish Highlands are just opening up.

Cities are the easy win for a first proper break in a while: one base, no logistics, and you can do as much or as little as you like.
