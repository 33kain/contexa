How it works

Nothing happens until you ask.

You know where the conversation should go next. Writing that as a precise message is the part that costs you. CONTEXA skips the writing, not the deciding.

1

Install it and open a conversation on claude.ai.

2

When a reply finishes, one mascot chip appears above your message box. No request is made yet — nothing about the conversation has left the page.

3

Press it. CONTEXA reads your own messages from the whole session — not just the last reply — and offers up to four next moves, each already written in full.

4

Pick one. It lands in your message box, whole. Edit anything, send when you're ready.

Nothing is ever sent for you. Clicking a move costs nothing further — the button you pressed in step 3 already spent the one request. The four moves are independent: each is a complete ask on its own, and picking one discards the rest. A menu, not a checklist.

What actually happens on the page

Real captures of the shipped extension — not mockups.

The mascot arrives when a reply finishes. Nothing has been asked yet, and nothing has been spent.

Press it, and CONTEXA offers up to four independent next moves — earned by something actually said in the session, not filler.

Pick one, and the whole prompt lands in your message box — <paste here> slots and Assume: lines included. Still nothing sent; that's your call.

CONTEXA follows the host page's theme. It doesn't impose one.

Zero is a valid outcome. If a session has nothing open, CONTEXA shows nothing — no floor, no padded row, no suggestion whose only virtue is that it could be shown.

Never fake output. When something goes wrong — a daily limit reached, a network failure — CONTEXA says what actually happened. There is no canned-suggestion fallback.

Privacy, plainly

What leaves your browser, and when

This is the substance of the full policy, not a summary that quietly means something looser.

  • Runs only on claude.ai. It touches no other site.
  • Nothing is sent anywhere until you press the button. A reply you never ask about never leaves your browser.
  • When you do press it, CONTEXA sends your own messages from that conversation and the reply you just received. Claude's earlier replies are never sent.
  • Hard limits on how much that can be: at most 40 of your messages, 12,000 characters in total, and 6,000 characters of the reply. The backend enforces these itself.
  • Your conversation text is never stored. It's used to write the suggestions, then discarded.
  • No accounts, no profiles, no tracking, no analytics, no advertising — and nothing sold or shared.
  • Your API key, if you provide one, stays on your device and goes only to Anthropic.

Using it

Free, with no account

Hosted (default)

No account, no sign-up, no API key. 20 replies a day are free — that's how many answers you can ask about; picking a move costs nothing extra.

Your own API key

Add it in the extension's settings and the daily limit goes away. Requests then go straight from your browser to Anthropic — nothing passes through our server at all.
