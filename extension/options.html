Suggestions come from CONTEXA's service — no setup needed. Fair-use limit of 20 replies per day.

Advanced
What leaves your browser. To suggest next steps, CONTEXA sends your last message and Claude's reply for that one exchange. With your own key, it goes directly to the Anthropic API and nowhere else. Without a key, it goes to CONTEXA's backend, which forwards it to Anthropic and returns the suggestions — the text is not stored, and there is no account or profile attached to it. Nothing is sent until a reply finishes, and never from a conversation you don't send a message in.
