How it works

1

Open a chat on claude.ai and ask anything.

2

When the answer finishes, CONTEXA asks you a short question just above your message box.

One at a time, never more than four, with the answers already written for you. Pick one, or skip it. If the answer left nothing worth asking, it stays quiet.

3

When you've answered, it writes the whole prompt into your message box.

You can edit it. Nothing is ever sent without you pressing send. If the conversation already settled something, CONTEXA writes that down as a line starting “Assume:” instead of asking you about it — change it or delete it before you send.

4

Or click ✎ Type & create magic and type roughly what you want.

Type "make it shorter" and CONTEXA writes the proper version for you.

Free. No account, no sign-up. Fair use is 10 prompts a day.
