What it actually does

Five rules the product doesn't break.

✦

Nothing happens until you ask

When a reply finishes, one small chip appears above your message box. No request, no model call, nothing about your conversation leaves the page — until you press it.

✦

It reads what you said, not just what Claude said

A follow-up written from the last answer alone is mostly about the last answer. CONTEXA reads your own messages across the whole conversation and mines the next move from that — Claude's reply is material, not the subject.

✦

A menu, not a checklist

Each move is a complete prompt on its own — one ask, ready to send. The moves don't run in order and don't build on each other. Picking one throws the rest away.

✦

When it has nothing to say, it says nothing

Every move has to be earned by something actually said in the conversation. Nothing earned, nothing offered — no filler, no padded row, no suggestion whose only virtue is that it could be shown.

✦

What it will not do

It will not rewrite what you typed, grade your prompts, or overlay your message box. It will not send anything on your behalf, ever — and when something goes wrong, it tells you what happened instead of quietly faking a suggestion.

How it works

Four steps. The fourth is always yours.

1

Install it, and open a conversation on claude.ai.

2

When a reply finishes, press the CONTEXA mascot above your message box.

3

Pick one of up to four prompts it offers. It lands in your message box, whole.

4

Edit if you want. Send when you're ready. CONTEXA never sends anything for you.

It follows your theme, not its own. Light or dark, CONTEXA reads whatever claude.ai is already rendering — nothing here imposes its own look on the page you're in.

Privacy, plainly

It sends less than you'd think, and never without asking.

  • Runs only on claude.ai. It touches no other site.
  • Nothing is sent until you click. A reply you never ask about never leaves your browser.
  • Sends only your own messages from that conversation, plus the reply you just got. Claude's earlier replies are never sent.
  • Conversation text is never stored. It's used to write the moves, then discarded.
  • No accounts, no profiles, no tracking, no analytics, no ads — and nothing sold or shared.
  • Your own API key, if you add one, stays on your device and goes only to Anthropic — bypassing our server entirely.
