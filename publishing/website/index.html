How it works

Three steps. No new habits.

CONTEXA works right inside your Claude chat. You stay in flow — it just hands you the next move.

1

Claude replies

When a reply finishes, CONTEXA quietly appears above the message box.

2

You get a menu

Up to four good next moves show up as clickable chips — read for context.

3

Pick one

Your choice lands in the box, unsent. Edit it if you like, then send.
