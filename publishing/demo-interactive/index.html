What just happened

Mined from your words

Every move is earned by a fragment of what you wrote, quoted verbatim. A move that cannot quote you is dropped before you see it — which is why the reply on screen is material, never the subject.

Two gates, both real

The evidence check runs, then the action gate: a label that does not open with a doable verb is dropped, and one that produces another question rather than work is dropped too. The list is the extension's own, English and Serbian.

Zero is a valid outcome

There is no floor and no filler. Type three vague words and the card says so instead of inventing four moves. The count under the stage is the honest one.

The other control: Start fresh

The row of moves is a way into the thread you are in. This is the way out of it. Every message you send on claude.ai is processed with the whole conversation behind it, so on a long thread the cost of the next message is the size of everything above it — and it is paid again on the one after. Past about 12,000 tokens the card says that number and offers one control. Below is a thread that got there.

What just happened here

The thread is invented. The number is not.

Nobody types a 58-message session into a demo, so this one is written — like the conversation in the figure on the site. The number beside CONTEXA is counted, not claimed: every character the page holds, divided by four, which is the estimate the extension makes on a real page. Open the fold and count them.

The brief is cut from the thread, not written about it.

No model here either. Every line is a run of words from a message above it, picked by rule: a clause naming a choice or a number becomes a settled fact, one naming something built becomes what exists now, and material you must bring along ends in <paste here>. The page adds the four labels and the bullets. The extension writes it with a model, under a prompt whose first rule is the one these rules cannot break anyway: nothing the session did not say.

Below the threshold, nothing.

The same gate runs on the card at the top of this page and draws nothing at all, because one message is sixty tokens rather than twelve thousand. And a heavy thread that settled nothing earns an empty brief: the card says Nothing to carry over rather than inventing a summary.

Open a new chat with it does not open claude.ai from here. The stage becomes the new conversation, with the brief in its message box, unsent — which is where the extension leaves you, in a real new tab. Nothing was deleted: the old thread is still there, and what changed is what the next message costs.

What the extension adds

  • The whole session, not one message. It reads every message you wrote in the conversation, oldest pinned first, and offers up to four independent moves.
  • The exit from a heavy thread is on this page now, one section up. What the page cannot show is the measurement — on one live session the same five messages cost 9% of the five-hour limit in a 689k-token thread and 3% after the brief, and across five threads the brief landed between 300 and 450 tokens whatever the thread weighed — or the model that writes it, in whatever language the session is already in. On a Cowork session it opens a new session in the same project instead of a new chat.
  • Nothing sent until you click. A reply nobody asks about costs nothing and never leaves the page; clicking a move costs no further model call.

The demo writes its moves from templates chosen by keyword, filled with your own words, and passed through the two gates above. The extension writes them with a model, against your whole session, and passes them through the same gates.
