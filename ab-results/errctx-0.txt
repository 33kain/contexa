Faults fixed (5)

1. extension/content.js — fitTurns() dropped `turns.splice(0, 1)`, i.e. the OLDEST turn including the pinned first one, so turn one was decapitated by the trim (the exact failure mode CLAUDE.md warns about). Changed to `turns.splice(1, 1)` so the first turn is pinned and the oldest MIDDLE turn goes first.
2. extension/background.js — takeBrief() had the staleness comparison inverted: `Date.now() - Number(p.t || 0) < BRIEF_TTL_MS` returned an empty brief for a FRESH brief and handed back a stale one. Changed `<` to `>`.
3. worker/src/index.js — `REPLIES_PER_DAY = 25` contradicted the single-source-of-truth daily twenty (and DEVICE_DAILY_LIMIT derives from it). Changed back to `20`.
4. worker/src/index.js — enforceAction() tested `ACTION_OPENERS` against `label.replace(/^\w+\s*/, '')`, stripping the label's first word — exactly the imperative verb the gate exists to check — so good rows were dropped and the gate stopped matching the byte-identical helper block in background.js. Changed back to `ACTION_OPENERS.test(label)`.
5. worker/src/index.js — admit() clamped the reply with `.slice(0, MIN_REPLY_CHARS)` (50) instead of the ceiling, truncating every reply to 50 chars and then failing its own `reply.trim().length < MIN_REPLY_CHARS` check. Changed to `.slice(0, MAX_REPLY_CHARS)`.

`npm test` was run 3 times in full (once to read the failures, once to list them, once to confirm the whole suite green). `npm run build` was also run once and reports the shared prompt / cachedSystem / usageOf invariants intact.
